EduNexus Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Effective Date: April 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your name, institutional email, student/faculty ID, and phone number. We also collect academic data such as attendance records, assignment submissions, grades, and fee payment statuses as part of the core functionality of the EduNexus ERP system.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for academic and institutional management. This includes:

  • Tracking and visualizing attendance behavior.
  • Managing academic grades, coursework, and timetables.
  • Facilitating secure login via email, institutional ID, or Google Sign-In.
  • Processing fee transactions and maintaining financial records.

3. Data Security and Storage

Your data is securely stored using industry-standard encryption protocols via Google Firebase. We implement stringent security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal and academic information.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may use third-party services (like Google Drive for assignment submissions) which are subject to their own respective privacy policies.

5. Terms of Use & User Conduct

By using the EduNexus ERP, you agree to:

  • Use the system only for legitimate academic purposes.
  • Not upload malicious files or scripts into the assignment submission portals.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of your login credentials.
  • Not attempt to reverse-engineer, manipulate, or scrape the dashboard APIs.
